AI Reel Maker - third-party software
====================================

AI Reel Maker itself is released under the MIT License (see LICENSE).
This installer also contains, or downloads when you ask for it, the following software.

Included in the installer
-------------------------

FFmpeg 9.0 (tools\ffmpeg)
  Full build by gyan.dev (https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/), licensed under the GNU General
  Public License version 3 (see tools\ffmpeg\LICENSE.txt). AI Reel Maker runs ffmpeg.exe and
  ffprobe.exe as separate programs. Source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html and
  https://github.com/GyanD/codexffmpeg (build scripts and the exact source of this build).

Node.js 24 (tools\node)
  MIT License and the licences of its bundled components: tools\node\LICENSE.
  https://nodejs.org

Python 3.12 (runtime\python)
  Python Software Foundation License: https://docs.python.org/3/license.html

Python packages (runtime\python\Lib\site-packages)
  Each package's licence is in its *.dist-info folder. They include Flask, Flask-SocketIO,
  NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, OpenCV (Apache 2.0), PyAV (BSD; contains FFmpeg libraries
  under the LGPL/GPL), faster-whisper and CTranslate2 (MIT), ONNX Runtime (MIT),
  kokoro-onnx (MIT) with espeak-ng (GPL v3, espeakng_loader), PySceneDetect (BSD), Pillow,
  SQLAlchemy, pydantic and huggingface_hub.

Downloaded only when you press a button in Settings
---------------------------------------------------

Remotion (Settings > Narrated videos > Prepare video tools)
  Installed from npm into %LOCALAPPDATA%\AI Reel Maker\remotion. Remotion is free for
  individuals, non-profits and companies of up to three people; larger companies need a
  company licence. See https://www.remotion.dev/license before using it for such a company.
  It also downloads a headless Chrome build (Chrome Headless Shell, Google).

AI models (Settings)
  Whisper speech models (MIT), Kokoro voice (Apache 2.0), SigLIP and CLAP models: each from
  Hugging Face under its own licence, stored in your user folder (.cache\huggingface).

Not included
------------

Ollama (https://ollama.com) and the Claude Code CLI (https://claude.com/claude-code) are
installed separately. The app uses them when they are on this computer.
